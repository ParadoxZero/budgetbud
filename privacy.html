Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This document outlines how we handle your data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data except for your user ID associated with Google or GitHub. This ID is used solely for authentication purposes.

We don't store any personal information. The unique google user ID we collect is stored as a cryptographic hash to identify users within budgetbud. As such, there is no way to reverse engineer the user ID from the stored data.

Cookies

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for authentication. No tracking cookies or other types of cookies are used.

Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with any third-party services except for authentication providers (Google or GitHub).

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at contact@budgetbud.app
